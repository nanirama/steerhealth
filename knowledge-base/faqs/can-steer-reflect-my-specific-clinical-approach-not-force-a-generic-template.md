# Can Steer reflect my specific clinical approach, not force a generic template?

Steer is designed to adapt to your style: you can create custom intakes, protocols, visit types, and recommendations that match your FM philosophy. AI-assisted notes and care plans learn from your language instead of forcing you into rigid templates.
