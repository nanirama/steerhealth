# How does Steer manage advanced labs (DUTCH, Genova, GI-MAP, etc.) and results?

Steer centralizes lab ordering, tracking, and result review so you’re not chasing PDFs and portals. Results can be tied directly to protocols and care plans, so your clinical decisions and automations are driven by the labs you already trust.
