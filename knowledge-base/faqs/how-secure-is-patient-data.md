# How secure is patient data?

Security isn’t just a priority at Steer Health; it’s our cornerstone. Steer Health is a HIPAA-compliant and SOC2-certified all-in-one platform that, additionally to securing its data with the highest industry standards available, does not share patient data with third parties or systems. This means it is more robust against phishing, unauthorized data sharing, and data leaks.
