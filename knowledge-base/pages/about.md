# About Us

## About Steer

Steer Health is an AI-powered growth and automation platform for healthcare. We help providers open up access, streamline operations, and deliver empathetic, high-quality experiences, so teams can focus on care, not busywork.

## Vision And Mission

### Our Mission

To make healthcare instantly accessible by eliminating the operational barriers that delay treatment, lose patients, and burn out medical staff.

### Our Vision

A world where no patient is lost to a voicemail or missed referral, every symptom reaches the right specialist within hours, providers focus on care instead of paperwork, and organizations generate revenue without human intervention.

## Who We Serve

Health systems, Medical Groups, ambulatory surgery centers, urgent care and ER networks, and specialty practices seeking to fill schedules, reduce administrative burden, and elevate patient experience.

## What We Do

The Steer Platform brings together three pillars to drive measurable outcomes

- **Digital Discovery & Access**: Modern DFD directs patients to the right provider, service, or location with real-time scheduling across all care venues from urgent care & ER to PCP, specialty, surgery, ASC, radiology & wayfinding.
- **Intelligent Engagement**: Pre-visit and post-visit automation, reminders, eligibility checks, payments, reputation management, and personalized outreach that retain patients and reduce no-shows.
- **Clinical & Ops Automation**: AI voice and messaging to deflect calls and speed resolution, along with documentation tools (e.g., AI notes and chart prep) that return time to clinicians and staff.

## Our Values

- **Patient Outcomes First**: Every decision is measured by whether it improves patient access, safety, or care. Revenue follows outcomes.
- **Relentless Focus**: We say no to good opportunities so we can say yes to great ones. We finish what we start before starting something new.
- **Own the Outcome**: We never pass problems up, sideways, or down. We solve them, or we escalate with a proposed solution.
- **Build Trust Through Transparency**: We share bad news fast. We give honest timelines. We are upfront about what we can and cannot do.
- **Speed With Precision**: We move fast on decisions and slow on clinical safety. We ship weekly and validate continuously.
- **Serve Relentlessly**: We exist to serve our patients, customers, and each other. We anticipate needs, follow through without reminders, and treat everyone's time as sacred.

## Our Story

- **Founded** — 2021: Steer Health is founded by Sridhar Yerramreddy, an IT and healthcare innovator with 23+ years of experience, inspired by his family of physicians to transform care with AI.
- **Strategic Partnership** — 2022: Prime Healthcare, one of the nation’s leading hospital systems, partners with Steer Health to accelerate patient engagement and operational efficiency.
- **Industry Recognition** — 2023: KLAS Emerging Solutions Spotlight Report names Steer Health a Top 20 Emerging Solutions Provider, highlighting measurable improvements in patient experience.
- **Product Breakthroughs** — 2024: Steer launches SteerNotes (AI scribe), Luna (AI voice agent), Steer Kiosk, and its AI Front Door expanding its platform for patient acquisition, retention,
- **Rapid Expansion** — 2025: Now serving 19M+ patients and 360+ organizations, Steer Health also enters functional medicine with partnerships including Forum Health and P2P.
