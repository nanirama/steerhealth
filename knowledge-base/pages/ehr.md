# EHR

## What We’re Trying to Solve

The healthcare system is under pressure, and it shows. Margins are shrinking. Costs are rising. Staff and clinicians are burned out. Steer Health was built to solve these problems at the root.

## Problem by the Numbers

Its bi-directional integration creates truly partnered workflows like:

- **60%** Physicians report feeling emotionally exhausted — Steer automates documentation, follow-up, and admin workflows so teams can focus on care—not clicks. — Clinician Burnout
- **15-20%** Admin and labor costs are rising year over year — Our platform reduces staffing inefficiencies and consolidates tools into one AI-driven ecosystem. — Escalating Operational Costs
- **All** Operating margins across U.S. hospitals are at historic lows — Steer unlocks revenue with intelligent scheduling, automation, and higher care capacity per provider. — Margin Compression

## How Steer Solves It

Steer is committed to the gold standard in information security and technical support, so you can serve your patients with confidence in your technology.

- **Built for ROI**
Every product in the Steer platform is designed to generate measurable growth, from net new patient acquisition to faster collections and reduced no-shows.
- **Powered by AI**
We eliminate manual tasks through AI-driven triage, intake, communications, and note-taking, giving time back to both staff and clinicians.
- **Seamless, Not Disruptive**
Steer integrates with your existing EHRs and workflows. No need to replace your current systems or retrain your team from scratch.
- **Results That Scale**
From solo practices to multi-hospital networks, Steer delivers scalable impact. Whether you’re expanding virtual care or reducing denials, we help you do more—with less.
