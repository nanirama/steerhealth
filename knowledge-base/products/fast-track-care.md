# Fast Track Care

## FastTrackCare

The seamless way for patients to discover care, book instantly, and get better.

## Key Statistics

- **72%** Tasks Automated
- **36%** Wait Reduced
- **24%** Visits Maximized

## How FastTrackCare Works

From reservation to arrival, every step designed to streamline care.

### Reserve your spot.

Scan a QR, tap a link, or text to select your ER arrival time,
no logins needed.

### Check in digitally.

Fill intake forms at home, then walk straight in when you arrive,
no waiting room delay.

### Stay informed in real time.

Get notifications if wait times change, patients always know
when to come in.

## Product Details

- **Search provider easily**: Quickly find and connect with the right provider for your needs.
- **Book your slot**: Secure your preferred time in just a few taps.

- **Recovery Information**: Access essential details to guide your healing journey.
- **Hold Your Place**: Complete intake from home, skip hours in the waiting room.
- **Steer does it all**: One platform to manage, connect, and grow effortlessly.
