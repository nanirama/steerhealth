## How Luna Works

From answering to refills, every call designed to resolve.

- **Answers in seconds.**: Luna greets callers, uses voice or chat to securely match them to  their EHR account, and pulls up relevant info instantly.
- **Schedules without effort.**: Whether it's scheduling, refills, FAQs, or routing, Luna processes requests naturally, and completes tasks via conversational interface.
- **Escalates with care.**: If a request needs human touch (e.g., urgent medical triage), Luna routes intelligently. For routine tasks, it resolves swiftly, streamlining operations and reducing staff workload

## Product Details

- **Free up your team, No burnout-More Care**: Matches callers to their Epic (or other) chart in seconds
- **Smart Scheduling**: Fills calendars & eliminates double-booking through real-time EHR slots

- **Rx Refills**: Patients check status or refill meds without waiting on staff
- **Unburden your team**: Go live in days no maintenance required
- **Omnichannel Reach**: Consistent experience across phone, web, SMS & chat
