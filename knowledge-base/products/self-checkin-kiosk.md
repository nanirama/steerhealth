# SteerKiosk

## SteerKiosk

Automated check-in that boosts revenue, reduces errors, and saves hours.

## Key Statistics

- **50%** Cash Flow Up
- **80%** Check in Faster
- **36%** Admin Reduced

## How does SteerKiosk Work

From arrival to access, every step designed to accelerate care.

### Scan or tap to begin.

Patients use smartphones or kiosks to start check-in,
no logins needed.

### Autofill & pay with ease.

Fill intake forms at home, then walk straight in when you arrive,
no waiting room delay.

### Step right in.

With registrations handled, patients bypass lines and proceed
directly to care.

## Product Details

- **Appointment Scheduling**: Patients can choose between online, SMS and Chatbot scheduling options for their appointments.
- **Reminders**: Patients receive automated appointment notifications and confirmation prompts.

- **Pre-registration and clinical intake**: Patients can scan insurance cards & IDs.
- **Staff Dashboard**: Staff can see patient overviews in the dashboard.
- **Kiosk Check-in**: The patient checks in and pays their point-of-service responsibility.

## Follow-up

After the patient visit, patients receive automated follow-up messages and re-engagement campaigns to drive customer loyalty.
