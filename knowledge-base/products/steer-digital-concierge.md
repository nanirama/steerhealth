# Steer Concierge

## Steer Concierge

Automated check-in that boosts revenue, reduces errors, and saves hours.

## How does Steer Concierge Works

From request to resolution, every interaction designed to care.

- **Send requests easily.**: Patients request meals, meds, or support directly via the Concierge app, no staff call needed.
- **Track and respond instantly.**: Staff view incoming patient needs on a dashboard and respond immediately, streamlined by AI.
- **Capture insights continuously.**: Every request is tracked, reported, and analyzed—helping teams refine care and reduce bottlenecks.

## Product Details

- **Free up your team, No burnout-More Care**: Matches callers to their Epic (or other) chart in seconds
- **Smart Scheduling**: Fills calendars & eliminates double-booking through real-time EHR slots

- **Rx Refills**: Patients check status or refill meds without waiting on staff
- **Unburden your team**: Go live in days no maintenance required
- **Omnichannel Reach**: Consistent experience across phone, web, SMS & chat
